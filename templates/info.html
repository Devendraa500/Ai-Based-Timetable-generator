about the project

the ai-based timetable generator is designed to automate scheduling of classes, teachers, and rooms efficiently using intelligent logic and constraints. it handles complex multi-year, multi-department scheduling with batch-wise lab allocation, faculty workload balancing, and room capacity enforcement.
